Irish Revenue Med 1 Tax Relief & Insurance Claimback

How Irish Patients Save Up to 70% on Fees

In Ireland, private healthcare expenses qualify for 20% standard tax relief via the Revenue Commissioner Med 1 scheme. Furthermore, policies with VHI, Laya Healthcare, Irish Life Health and Garda Medical Aid provide direct rebates on outpatient consultations. Our clinic receipts contain all required IMC registration codes for one-click claim submission.
